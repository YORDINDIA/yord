# Marketing and Growth Automations

## Goals
- Grow traffic and conversions with lightweight, AI-assisted workflows.

## Suggested Automations
- SEO audit: detect missing tags, thin descriptions, duplicate handles.
- Featured product rotation based on recent sales.
- Cross-sell bundles based on line-item co-occurrence.
- Collection gap detection: products without a collection.
- Content calendar: cadence reminders and suggested topics.

## Outputs
- Each automation produces a suggestion list, not direct writes.
- Requires admin approval to apply changes.
